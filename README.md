# 📊 Amazon Product Analysis

![Project Banner](projectbanner.png.jpeg)

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes Amazon product data using Python to uncover insights into pricing, discounts, customer ratings, and product categories. The analysis includes data cleaning, exploratory data analysis (EDA), data visualization, and business recommendations.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

The goal of this project is to answer key business questions such as:

- Which product categories have the highest ratings?
- Do higher discounts lead to better customer ratings?
- Which categories receive the biggest price reductions?
- What insights can businesses use to improve decision-making?

---

## 📂 Dataset

- **Dataset:** Amazon Product Dataset
- **Records:** 1,464 products
- **Features:** Product Name, Category, Price, Discount, Rating, Rating Count, Reviews, etc.

## 💡 Key Insights

- Most products have ratings between **4.0 and 4.4**.
- Higher discounts do not necessarily result in higher customer ratings.
- Electronics and Computers & Accessories dominate the dataset.
- Actual Price and Discounted Price have a very strong positive correlation.
- Customer satisfaction appears to depend more on product quality than discount percentage.

---

## 📈 Business Recommendations

- Focus on product quality instead of relying only on heavy discounts.
- Promote highly rated categories in marketing campaigns.
- Monitor customer reviews to improve customer satisfaction.
- Review products with large discounts but low ratings.
